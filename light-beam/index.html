About the game

Light Beam is a relaxing laser puzzle about bending light. Tap mirrors to turn them, guide the beam across the board and light up every target to solve the level.

It starts with a single beam and two mirrors. Then the light splits through glass, breaks into red, green and blue in a prism, jumps through portals and opens gates - and every level becomes a real brain teaser.

No timers, no lives, no energy: think as long as you like. Stuck? Tap the light bulb and the game circles a piece that is turned the wrong way.

  • 200 levels, from two mirrors up to three beams and twenty-plus moves
  • A par on every level - solve it in par for all three stars
  • Mirrors, glass, prisms, color filters and color shifters
  • One-way glass, portals, switches, gates and dark sensors
  • No in-app purchases, and hints are always your choice
  • Glowing 3D lasers, rendered on our own engine

Privacy

Light Beam has no accounts, no in-app purchases and no server of its own, and we collect nothing about you. Your progress, stars and settings are kept on your device.

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising identifier and similar data to serve them. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any ad is loaded, and you can change your answer at any time.
