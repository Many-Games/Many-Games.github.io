About the game

It starts with the puzzle you know: move a pyramid of disks to another rod, one disk at a time, and never put a disk on a smaller one.

Then the colors come in. Every color is dealt out in a scramble across more and more rods, and you have to sort each one back into a tower of its own, in as few moves as you can. Later levels hide some of the disks until you dig down to them, and paint rods that will only take their own color.

Stuck? Undo a move, buy yourself an extra move, or bring in an extra rod.

  • 80 levels, from the classic three-rod puzzle up to eight colors over ten rods
  • A target number of moves to beat on every level
  • Hidden disks that show their color once you uncover them
  • Colored rods that only accept disks of their own color
  • Undo, extra moves and an extra rod when you need a hand
  • Rendered in 3D on our own engine

Privacy

Hanoi Tower Chaos has no accounts and no server of its own, and we collect nothing about you. Your progress and settings are kept on your device.

The game shows ads from Google AdMob, which collects your device's advertising identifier and similar data to serve them. In the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland the game asks for your consent before any ad is loaded, and you can change your answer at any time.
