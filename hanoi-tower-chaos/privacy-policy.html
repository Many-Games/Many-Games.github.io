Privacy Policy for Hanoi Tower Chaos

Last updated 30 July 2026

Hanoi Tower Chaos ("the game") is published by Amir Many ("the developer"). This policy explains what information the game handles, what leaves your device, and what choices you have. It applies to the Android version of the game distributed through Google Play.

What the developer collects

Nothing. The game has no accounts, no sign-in, no registration and no server of its own. The developer receives no personal information about you and cannot identify you.

The game stores your progress and settings - the level you have reached, your gold, and whether music and sound effects are switched on - in the app's own private storage on your device. That file never leaves the device except through a backup you yourself ask Android to make, and it is removed when you uninstall the game.

Advertising

The game shows video ads served by Google AdMob. They come in two kinds. The first you ask for: a video watched in exchange for an extra move, an extra rod or an undo, offered when you cannot otherwise afford the action and never required. The second plays by itself after every fifth level you finish or fail, and grants you nothing in return; it cannot be declined, although you can close it once it appears. When no ad has loaded, the game carries on without one.

To serve those ads, the Google Mobile Ads software in the game collects and shares the following with Google:

  • your device's advertising identifier
  • your Internet Protocol address
  • your interactions with the ads and with the game
  • diagnostic information about the device and the app

Google uses this for advertising, analytics and fraud prevention. This data is sent over an encrypted connection. The developer never sees it; it is handled by Google under Google's Privacy Policy and described further in How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services.

Consent

Where the law requires it - in the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom and Switzerland - the game asks for your consent before any ad is loaded, through a form from a Google-certified consent platform.

Nothing is requested from the advertising software until you have answered, and if you decline, no ads are served at all. Your answer is remembered between sessions and can be changed at any time from the Ad privacy options button on the game's Licenses screen. The game re-reads your answer each time you return to it, so consent taken back takes effect at once.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your advertising identifier. Android Settings, then Privacy, then Ads. Deleting it stops apps receiving an identifier at all.
  • Turn off ad personalisation. The same Android settings screen. You will still see ads, but they will not be based on your interests.
  • Decline the ads you are offered. The videos that buy an extra move, an extra rod or an undo are always optional. The ad that plays every fifth level is not.

Children

The game is not directed at children under 13, and the developer does not knowingly collect information from them.

Data retention and deletion

The developer holds no data about you, so there is nothing to request, export or delete. Your local progress is deleted when you uninstall the game. Data held by Google for advertising is subject to Google's own retention practices, and you can reset your advertising identifier at any time as described above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted at this address with a new date at the top. Material changes will also be noted in the game's release notes on Google Play.
